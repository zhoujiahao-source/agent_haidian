# Jing-Zhang City Learning Assembly｜京张城市学习大会

> Proposed annual civic operating ritual. It is **not** a claim that such an event has been approved, funded or scheduled.

## 1. Core purpose

The Assembly is the public accountability endpoint of the annual City OS cycle. Its central act is the **Annual City Version Release**, not a keynote or product expo.

The Assembly must answer:
- What urban problems did the city try to address?
- Which capabilities were Added, Modified, Scaled, Stopped or Retired?
- What public value changed, for whom, and with what confidence?
- What incidents, complaints, accessibility failures or unintended effects occurred?
- Which technologies or proposals did **not** deserve continuation?
- What remains unresolved?
- What should enter the next Problem Track?

## 2. Proposed program architecture

### Session A — City Version Release
Public Stewardship presents release notes with evidence links, uncertainty and accountable owners. Responsible STOPs and retirements receive equal visibility with additions.

### Session B — Public Value Review
Professional/research evaluators explain changes in Urban Adaptation Rate and scenario metrics, including distributional results, outliers, complaints and human takeover. No average-only success narrative.

### Session C — Incident & Failure Hearing
Material incidents and failed/modified trials are reviewed in a learning format. The aim is neither blame avoidance nor celebratory “failure culture”; it is to show what failed, who was responsible for response, what changed and whether affected people received a remedy/alternative where applicable.

### Session D — Community Objection & Minority Report
Unresolved objections, vulnerable-group concerns and minority expert/community views receive a visible place. A disagreement can remain open rather than being flattened into false consensus.

### Session E — Capability Maintenance & Migration Clinic
Maintainers report end-of-support risks, dependency changes, interoperability tests and planned retirements. This prevents the annual event from rewarding only new launches.

### Session F — Next-Year Challenge Deliberation
Unresolved urban problems are converted into candidate Problem Track briefs. Technology suppliers do not define the problem by default.

### Session G — Landmark as Memory / Retired Capability Archive
A physical-first public exhibition/update records successful, modified, stopped and retired capabilities along the 1909 → 2026 → Future memory structure. Digital media supports, rather than replaces, place-based public memory.

## 3. Required public artifacts

- current City Version Release notes;
- machine/human-readable capability status list;
- public-value metric change summary with confidence/limitations;
- incident/lesson summary appropriate for lawful public disclosure;
- complaints/objections and response-status summary;
- Retired Capability Archive additions;
- unresolved-problem register;
- next-year challenge candidates;
- audit/source links;
- plain-language summary and accessible formats.

## 4. Participation architecture

Proposed participant roles:
- accountable public operator/steward;
- professional reviewers;
- research/evaluation teams;
- maintainers/developers;
- community and affected-group representatives;
- independent/public observers;
- general public.

No named real institution is assigned in this workstream.

## 5. Anti-expo guardrails

The Assembly fails if:
- the main KPI is attendance/media exposure;
- vendors dominate the agenda;
- only new products are shown;
- STOPs/retirements/incidents are hidden;
- unresolved objections are excluded;
- participation happens only at the event;
- it implies government endorsement of proposals merely because they are displayed;
- sponsorship or branding overrides public-interest disclosure.

## 6. International layer

A future global city-learning network is a valid **proposed** extension: peer cities/researchers could compare methods for public-value evaluation, reversible urban AI trials, capability registries, retirement and interoperability. Any real partnership must be separately verified and must never be written as signed/confirmed without evidence.

## 7. Success test for the Assembly

The event succeeds if a person can leave knowing not only what technology appeared, but **what the city learned, what it stopped, what changed because people objected, what remains unresolved, and who is accountable for the next version**.
