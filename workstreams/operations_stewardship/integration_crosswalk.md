## 7. Full operating logic chain

The Window 6 implementation of the master logic chain is:

1. **Site / city context** → use only repository-approved/public/cleared evidence; geometry limitations remain visible.
2. **Resident/user need** → Problem Track records affected groups and baseline.
3. **Planning problem** → define the public-value objective before choosing technology.
4. **Non-AI comparator** → test simpler physical/service/process alternatives.
5. **Capability candidate** → enter through Capability Track or Problem Track solution review.
6. **Data/provenance** → record source, necessity, limits and legal screening.
7. **Risk/rights** → predeclare risk ceiling, human override, accessibility and contestability.
8. **Bounded reversible trial** → explicit site/time/population/stop conditions.
9. **Measurement** → scenario metrics + common safeguard floors.
10. **Decision** → STOP / MODIFY / CONTINUE / SCALE with evidence.
11. **Registry/version** → Capability Registry records identity, owner, maintainer, dependencies, evidence and status.
12. **Maintenance** → lifecycle funding/support/migration/compatibility rather than “pilot then abandon.”
13. **Annual public release** → City Version Release publishes adds/modifications/scales/stops/retirements/incidents/objections/Unknowns.
14. **Retirement** → service continuity, shutdown, credential/data/device disposition and tombstone.
15. **Archive/learning** → Retired Capability Archive preserves public lessons/provenance.
16. **Next cycle** → unresolved or newly observed problems return to Problem Track.

## 10. Status language crosswalk

Use:
- `Official`: only where a repository-approved official source supports the fact.
- `Observed/Derived`: where reproducible evidence/calculation supports it.
- `Proposed`: Window 6 institutional/process/spatial operating suggestions.
- `Assumed`: explicit design assumption with consequence.
- `Unknown`: missing authority, budget, threshold, legal mapping, official geometry/control or real deployment data.

Do not use:
- “the government will establish PSO”;
- “the city has approved the sandbox”;
- “residents consent through participation”;
- “this is the statutory appeal process”;
- “this capability will be deployed”;
- “the annual assembly is officially scheduled”;
- “UAR is an adopted government KPI.”
