## Highest-priority integration risks

1. **Do not let operational language imply a real government organizational chart.** Keep PSO/review pool names as functional placeholders unless a competent real institution is verified.
2. **Do not copy exact numeric risk thresholds into the final proposal without a source.** This workstream intentionally defines gates and decision logic, not pseudo-official cutoffs.
3. **Resolve the current repository wording conflict around provisional geometry/content scoring at integration.** Current Skill/rubric say organizer-missing official polygons do not themselves block content scoring; a stale design-brief boolean appears stricter.
4. **Keep Public Stewardship separate from vendor governance.** A vendor can be Responsible for build/maintenance but not Accountable for public authorization.
5. **Preserve failure visibility.** Removing Responsible STOPs, incidents and retirements from the annual release would break the concept's public-learning logic.

## Risk review cadence

- each capability admission: capability-specific institutional/maintenance risks;
- quarterly: systemic patterns across active capabilities;
- annual: institutional-risk review before City Version Release;
- after material incident: immediate review of whether the problem is capability-specific or systemic;
- every major technology/procurement transition: lock-in, continuity and migration review.
