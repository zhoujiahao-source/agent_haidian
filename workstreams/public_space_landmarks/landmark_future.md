# Landmark Future｜未来开放实验站 / Future Open Test Station

## 1. Landmark proposition

The Future landmark is not a museum about predicted technologies. It is a **permanently unfinished civic test station** where unresolved urban problems can enter small, reversible, monitored experiments under clear authorization.

Its physical identity should visibly communicate: **temporary, adjustable, stoppable, accountable.**

## 2. What enters the station

A candidate experiment must begin with an unresolved public problem, not a technology seeking a showcase. Entry requires:

1. a named public problem and target beneficiaries;
2. evidence that ordinary spatial/service measures have been considered first;
3. risk classification and required human/professional authorization;
4. a bounded place and time window;
5. nonparticipant bypass / non-AI baseline;
6. minimum necessary data plan;
7. named human stop authority;
8. predeclared success and STOP conditions;
9. a removal/recovery plan;
10. a path back to the 2026 City Learning Archive for review.

## 3. Spatial composition

### A. Problem Porch

A public-facing edge shows a small set of **unresolved questions**, not product advertisements. Each question includes who is affected, what is already known, what is Unknown and whether a test has been authorized.

### B. Reversible Bays

Modular bays support temporary physical/service experiments. Their exact engineering system is left for professional design. The important spatial rules are:

- removable rather than permanent where possible;
- ordinary circulation remains independent;
- service/maintenance access is explicit;
- observation does not force participation;
- barriers/status signs make operating state legible;
- a safe shutdown state is physically designed.

### C. Observation Edge

Residents, professionals and operators can inspect a trial without entering the controlled zone. It supports briefings, accessibility observation, public-value monitoring and after-action discussion.

### D. Manual Control / Steward Point

The station visibly contains human operational responsibility. The goal is not to theatricalize an emergency stop button, but to make real stop authority, contact and procedure clear.

### E. Reset Yard

A practical storage/service area supports removal, repair, reconfiguration and safe disposal. **Reset capacity is part of the landmark identity**: the city demonstrates that experiments can leave.

## 4. Public Capability Interface role

Dominant typology: **N5 Reversible Test Yard**.

Supporting nodes/capabilities:
- N7 Continuity Island for degraded/emergency mode;
- N8 Capability Interchange for transferring mature capabilities to/from other learning units;
- CAP-06 Try & Learn;
- CAP-08 Emergency Continuity;
- CAP-09 Capability Exchange.

## 5. Ambient / Consent / Human Override

- **Ambient:** equipment/environmental status required for safe operation may run within the approved trial boundary.
- **Consent:** explicit participation is required when a trial interacts with personal data, individualized service or a participant's behavior beyond ordinary non-personal public-space observation.
- **Nonparticipant right:** a person can use the surrounding public realm without becoming a test subject.
- **Human override:** a named trained operator/professional team has stop authority; higher-risk authorization remains with the relevant competent human/public institution.
- **Failure-safe:** test equipment goes to a safe state; ordinary routes and emergency procedures remain independent.

## 6. No “future theater” rule

The station rejects:

- concept cars/robots placed as static props;
- speculative gadgets with no public problem;
- immersive screens that substitute for real-world evidence;
- permanent construction justified by a short-lived technology;
- tests whose risks cannot be bounded or reversed;
- participation by default because someone walked through a public space.

## 7. Trial lifecycle displayed in space

`UNRESOLVED PROBLEM → PROPOSED TEST → AUTHORIZED / NOT AUTHORIZED → ACTIVE WINDOW → PAUSED / STOPPED / COMPLETED → PUBLIC VALUE REVIEW → MODIFY / CONTINUE / SCALE / RETIRE`

The current state is expressed with durable physical signage plus optional live digital status. A stopped bay is not visually treated as a failure; its record moves to the 2026 archive and, when significant, the Retired Capability Archive.

## 8. Relationship to the five learning units

The Future station can be a **networked landmark** rather than one building:

- controlled physical-world test capacity is strongest in Zhongzhiyuan;
- public-space/climate prototypes may appear in selected Xiaoyuehe segments;
- service/business prototypes may appear in suitable Dazhongsi premises;
- community co-creation and feedback occurs in AI Origin Community;
- standards/transfer support connects through Zhongguancun Technology Service Wing.

The landmark identity is therefore the common reversible-test protocol and spatial kit, with one primary anchor to be selected after verified spatial/operational assessment.

## 9. Material / visual language

- exposed but orderly modular joints;
- clear service edges and reset/storage logic;
- paired-line / switch graphics used for state and routing, not nostalgia;
- neutral civic materials and public-service typography;
- no blue-purple neon, giant robot, chip icon or glowing-brain imagery.

## 10. Link to retirement

The Future station is incomplete without a visible exit route. When a capability is superseded, unsafe, low-value, too costly, inequitable or no longer supportable, the physical prototype is removed/reused/recycled safely and its public lesson enters the Retired Capability Archive.

## Phase 7 checkpoint

- **Completed:** Future defined as a reversible experimentation institution and spatial kit rather than a prediction gallery.
- **Found:** reset/removal capacity is as important as test capacity; visible incompleteness is a governance feature.
- **Unknown:** exact anchor site, engineering utilities, test categories, permitting pathway, stewardship and operating budget.
- **Next dependency:** define a dignified retirement/archive system that preserves lessons while deleting unnecessary data and safely handling physical artifacts.
