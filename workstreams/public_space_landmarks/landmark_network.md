# Landmark Network｜1909 → 2026 → Future → Retired

## Core idea

The landmark system is **one continuous civic-learning narrative distributed through the Capability Backbone**, not three isolated icon buildings.

`1909 Railway Memory Ground → 2026 City Learning Archive → Future Open Test Station → Retired Capability Archive → back to new urban problems`

## Four roles

| Time / state | Spatial role | Public question | Dominant node | What must remain physical |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1909** | memory ground | How did a capability become public infrastructure? | N4 Memory Switch | verified historical narrative, route, material/place memory |
| **2026** | learning archive | How does the city decide to change/stop/scale a capability? | N6 + N8 | version record, evidence/decision cards, public review place |
| **Future** | reversible experiment | Which unresolved problem deserves a bounded test? | N5 | bypass, test boundary, status, human stop/reset capacity |
| **Retired** | responsible exit memory | Why did a capability leave, and what should replace it? | N4 archive layer | retirement label, selected safe artifact, replacement/lesson record |

## Continuity device

The common visual DNA is intentionally limited:

- **paired rails:** public-space baseline + optional capability layer;
- **node:** place where capability/memory can be called;
- **switch:** choice, authorization, modification or exit;
- **mileage/version:** time and city-learning release;
- **archive tag:** stable public memory.

This creates continuity without turning the corridor into retro railway theming.

## Spatial distribution principle

The four roles do not require four large buildings.

- 1909 should attach to verified heritage/public-space fabric.
- 2026 should occupy an accessible civic gathering/archive position connected to daily life and public review.
- Future may operate as a network with the strongest controlled anchor in Zhongzhiyuan and satellite test contexts elsewhere.
- Retired records may be distributed at the 2026 archive and selected former-use sites, with only a small artifact collection.

Exact anchors are **Unknown / Proposed** until official geometry, heritage, ownership, movement and operational feasibility are verified.

## Daily-use requirement

Every landmark role must support ordinary public life:

- 1909: walking, sitting, orientation, culture;
- 2026: meeting, service explanation, civic review, capability exchange;
- Future: ordinary bypass/public observation plus professional test use;
- Retired: reading, resting, education and material-reuse interpretation.

A landmark that only works during a festival or only after opening an app is rejected.

## Relationship to the five zones

- **Zhongzhiyuan:** Future/test and transfer emphasis.
- **AI Origin Community:** 2026 public review/co-creation emphasis.
- **Dazhongsi:** service/business trial lessons feed 2026/Retired records.
- **Zhongguancun Service Wing:** capability/version transfer and professional exchange.
- **Xiaoyuehe:** landscape/public-space capability revalidation and everyday memory interfaces.

The Final Integration Agent should choose exact anchor locations from verified site evidence while preserving this functional differentiation.

## Narrative sentence for boards

**1909 taught the city to build capability; 2026 teaches it to judge capability; Future keeps testing what is unresolved; Retired remembers how to let capability leave responsibly.**
