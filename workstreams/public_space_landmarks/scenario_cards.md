## S01 — Morning Seam Crossing / 早高峰东西缝合
- **scenario_id:** S01
- **title:** Morning Seam Crossing
- **place:** a verified east-west barrier/crossing on the heritage-park/Capability Backbone interface
- **time:** weekday 07:30–09:30
- **persona:** P01 commuter; affected P04 disabled user, P07 frontline operator
- **urban_problem:** rail/road legacy seams, confusing transfers, crowding and crossing delay
- **public_capability:** CAP-01 Find & Ask + CAP-03 Access & Care
- **non-AI spatial solution:** continuous step-free path, safe crossing geometry, static/tactile wayfinding, lighting, queue space
- **AI role:** explain disruptions and synthesize optional route choices from public service data
- **ambient/consent:** ambient public status; consent only for saved/personal route preference
- **data_needed:** verified pedestrian/crossing geometry, public transport/service status, accessibility audit
- **risk:** R1–R2
- **human_override:** transport/site operations and staffed help contact
- **success_metrics:** journey completion, missed-turn rate, accessible-route parity, manual help demand, complaints
- **failure/stop:** disable personalized routing if unsafe/conflicting instructions recur; static system remains
- **physical_changes:** signs, tactile layer, rest/queue zone, crossing/interface improvements subject to professional design
- **provisional assumptions:** exact seam and footfall are Unknown
