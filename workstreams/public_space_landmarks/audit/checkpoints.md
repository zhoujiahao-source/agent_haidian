# Phase Checkpoints — WINDOW 4 Public Space & Landmarks

## Phase 0 — Public space / taskbook audit
- **Completed:** latest upstream Skill/taskbook/design-space/source/geometry status checked; historical user candidate audited.
- **Found:** prior 7 personas / 12 scenes / legibility controls are reusable, but old landmark logic does not satisfy the new 1909→2026→Future + retirement constitution.
- **Unknown:** official exact polygons, heritage GIS, ownership, statutory controls, utilities and detailed existing-condition survey.
- **Dependency:** build geometry-independent civic typologies first.

## Phase 1 — Public capability catalog
- **Completed:** 9 capability classes from real urban problems, each with non-AI baseline, AI role, risk/data boundary and stop logic.
- **Found:** heat/accessibility/service quality cannot be delegated to AI; physical city and staffing are the baseline.
- **Unknown:** measured demand/capacity and operating owner for each future capability.
- **Dependency:** translate capabilities into spatial node types.

## Phase 2 — Node family
- **Completed:** 8 node typologies and 20-component public-space library.
- **Found:** visual consistency can come from line/node/switch/version grammar without repeating a smart kiosk.
- **Unknown:** exact node count, dimensions, materials, products and placement.
- **Dependency:** test node family against diverse people/time periods.

## Phase 3 — Personas and time
- **Completed:** 8 evaluation personas covering commuter, older resident, child/caregiver, disabled user, visitor, developer/researcher, frontline operator, merchant/service operator.
- **Found:** frontline staff and people who opt out are essential evaluation personas, not edge cases.
- **Unknown:** demographic/usage distribution; personas are not population forecasts.
- **Dependency:** create governed scenarios with subgroup/failure tests.

## Phase 4 — Scenario orchestration
- **Completed:** 12 full scenario cards; 4 explicit testing/validation scenes; 24-hour + seasonal + emergency orchestration across all five learning units.
- **Found:** controlled tests must be bounded in both time and space, with ordinary bypass routes.
- **Unknown:** current pedestrian counts, thermal hotspots, night activity, accessibility barrier inventory.
- **Dependency:** connect daily scenarios to memory landmarks.

## Phase 5 — 1909 landmark
- **Completed:** Railway Memory Ground as an inhabitable physical heritage/public-space sequence; public Beijing sources checked.
- **Found:** strongest continuity is “capability embedded in public infrastructure,” not nostalgic railway imagery.
- **Unknown:** exact heritage-control boundary, object provenance, permitted interventions and anchor site.
- **Dependency:** shift from historic capability-building to contemporary city-learning memory.

## Phase 6 — 2026 landmark
- **Completed:** City Learning Archive with Annual City Version Release, Evidence Tables, Modification Wall, Stop Ledger, public review and non-hype honor system.
- **Found:** responsible STOP/MODIFY can be as valuable as SCALE and should receive equal public memory.
- **Unknown:** stewardship body, archive infrastructure, legal publication/retention process, exact site.
- **Dependency:** make unresolved problems testable rather than turn Future into a museum.

## Phase 7 — Future landmark
- **Completed:** Future Open Test Station with Problem Porch, reversible bays, public observation, bypass, manual steward point and reset yard.
- **Found:** visible reset/removal capacity is a core architectural/governance feature.
- **Unknown:** engineering systems, test categories, permitting, utilities, operating budget and exact anchor.
- **Dependency:** define responsible technological exit.

## Phase 8 — Retired Capability Archive
- **Completed:** retirement triggers, public archive label, data deletion/retention protocol, safe artifact handling and distributed spatial form.
- **Found:** deleting unnecessary data and safely removing artifacts are part of public memory.
- **Unknown:** statutory retention, artifact ownership, storage/disposal/reuse operators.
- **Dependency:** represent normal/failure/user journeys spatially.

## Phase 9 — Sections and experience sequence
- **Completed:** four programmatic sections and five user/lifecycle sequences; AI is explicitly backstage and failure-safe mode is drawn conceptually.
- **Found:** degraded/OFF sections are stronger evidence of public value than glossy normal-operation renders.
- **Unknown:** all survey/engineering dimensions and detailed standard compliance.
- **Dependency:** integration-stage mapping to verified basemap + production of evidence figures.

## Current completion status

The role-specific conceptual/design workstream is complete enough for Final Integration. It intentionally does **not** claim canonical submission modification, engineering completion, official landmark location or approved implementation.
