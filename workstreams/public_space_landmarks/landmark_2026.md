# Landmark 2026｜城市学习档案 / City Learning Archive

## 1. Landmark proposition

The 2026 landmark is a **public archive of how the city learns**, not an AI achievement hall. Its primary exhibit is the decision trail from urban problem to trial, evidence, public-value review and `STOP / MODIFY / CONTINUE / SCALE`.

Its civic message is deliberately unusual for a technology landmark:

> **A city that can publicly explain why it changed or stopped a technology is more mature than a city that only displays successful deployments.**

## 2. What is archived

Every eligible capability/trial has a compact public record:

- problem addressed;
- capability/version;
- place and period of the bounded trial;
- status: concept / trial / service / degraded / retired;
- steward and responsible human/organization role;
- risk class and authorization route;
- data class and retention summary;
- non-AI baseline / fallback;
- public-value evidence: safety, fairness, privacy, environment, cost + scenario-specific measures;
- complaints, manual takeovers, incidents/limitations and subgroup effects where publication is lawful and safe;
- decision: STOP / MODIFY / CONTINUE / SCALE;
- what changed after review;
- link to later cross-zone revalidation or retirement.

The archive does **not** publish personal records, raw sensitive logs or material that would create a privacy/security risk.

## 3. Spatial composition

### A. Version Hall — Annual City Version Release

A civic room/covered public-space edge publishes the annual capability change set, for example `Jing-Zhang City v2027`:

- Added
- Modified
- Scaled
- Stopped
- Retired
- Unresolved

The release is an accountable public record, not a software metaphor that replaces planning/governance language.

### B. Evidence Tables

Physical tables/walls let visitors inspect a small number of representative cases through comparable evidence cards. Digital layers may offer deeper filtering, but the core conclusion and status are physically readable.

### C. The Modification Wall

A dedicated surface shows before/after changes to selected trials. It gives **MODIFY** equal visual status to success and prevents the archive from becoming a trophy wall.

### D. The Stop Ledger

A calm, non-sensational record explains selected stopped trials: what was attempted, what evidence failed, what protected public value, and what was learned. It is explicitly not a “failure shame” installation.

### E. Public Review Table

A staffed/community-scale place supports periodic explanation sessions, review workshops and appeals/feedback. It connects to N6 Community Dock and CAP-07 Co-create & Appeal.

## 4. Public Capability Interface role

Dominant roles: **N6 Community Dock + N8 Capability Interchange + N4 Memory Switch**.

Capabilities:
- CAP-07 Co-create & Appeal;
- CAP-09 Capability Exchange;
- CAP-05 Memory & Meaning;
- CAP-01 Find & Ask.

This means the archive is operational: it helps people understand, contest, transfer and improve capabilities rather than only observe history.

## 5. Ambient / Consent / Human Override

- **Ambient:** public non-personal capability status and archive availability may update automatically.
- **Consent:** participation in feedback, personalized subscriptions or account-linked follow-up requires active action; anonymous/low-tech feedback remains available where feasible.
- **Human override:** Public Stewardship / responsible review body controls publication, correction, redaction and final decisions. AI may summarize but cannot erase original authorized inputs or decide the public verdict by itself.
- **Failure-safe:** printed/static version summaries, public meeting and staffed explanation remain usable offline.

## 6. Honor display system

The official taskbook asks for an honor-display system. Here, honor is separated from hype.

### What can be honored
- documented public-value improvement;
- responsible STOP that prevented harm;
- a useful modification triggered by community/frontline feedback;
- reusable open methods, standards or accessibility improvements;
- verified contributions by Agent, developer, resident, operator or professional teams.

### What cannot be implied
- repository merge = government approval;
- proposal = built project;
- technical benchmark = public value;
- contributor popularity = merit;
- company presence = official partnership.

Every honor record includes contribution, evidence/status and attribution/rights status.

## 7. Anti-gaming display rules

The archive avoids a single leaderboard. It pairs headline outcome indicators with:

- subgroup distributions;
- complaints and appeal outcomes;
- manual takeover counts/rates when meaningful;
- safety/near-miss records where publishable;
- resource use / side effects;
- reasons for STOP/MODIFY;
- data-quality/confidence notes.

Urban Adaptation Rate may appear as a flagship system indicator only when its formula, denominator, data window and limitations are explicit.

## 8. Privacy and retention

Archive publication follows a “public lesson, minimum personal data” rule:

1. publish the capability and governance lesson, not a person's trace;
2. aggregate or redact incident evidence where required;
3. keep source records only under the relevant lawful governance process;
4. when a capability retires, carry forward only the minimum justified record into the Retired Capability Archive;
5. visibly distinguish `Unknown / Assumed / Derived / Verified / Official` evidence status.

## 9. Relationship to old “Legible AI” assets

The prior Mile Zero / Legibility Observatory idea can be reused as **interface components** — status disclosure, operator identity, exit path, evidence label — but should not remain the primary landmark narrative. In the updated constitution, “legibility” serves the City Learning Archive rather than becoming the total concept.

## 10. Link to Future

The 2026 archive ends with an **Unresolved Problems Register**. Items do not become promises; they become candidates for the Future Open Test Station if a bounded, reversible and publicly reviewable experiment can be designed.

## Phase 6 checkpoint

- **Completed:** a physical-first 2026 landmark that records success, modification, stopping, unresolved problems and responsible contributors.
- **Found:** the honor system is stronger when it also recognizes responsible stopping and repair, not only expansion.
- **Unknown:** future stewardship institution, publication cadence/details, archive data infrastructure, legal retention requirements and exact site.
- **Next dependency:** give unresolved problems a reversible experimental place without turning “Future” into a static exhibition.
