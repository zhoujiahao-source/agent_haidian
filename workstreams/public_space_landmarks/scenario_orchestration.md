# Scenario Orchestration｜场景编排

## 1. Why orchestration, not a “smart plaza”

Public life changes by hour, season, user and risk. The proposal therefore distributes capabilities through a node network and lets the same corridor/space support different civic roles over time. No single plaza is asked to solve all needs.

The orchestration rule is:

**ordinary city first → low-risk ambient support → opt-in personalized service → bounded reversible experiment → public-value review → keep/modify/stop/scale.**

## 3. Spatial orchestration across the five learning units

### Zhongzhiyuan — Produce, test, stop safely

Dominant nodes: **N5 Reversible Test Yard, N8 Capability Interchange, N7 Continuity Island**.

- S10/S11 are deliberately confined to declared test windows and separated circulation.
- A successful test does not automatically become a public service; it must enter public-value review and be revalidated in another context before SCALE.
- Failed or stopped trials feed the 2026 City Learning Archive and, when historically meaningful, the Retired Capability Archive.

### Beijing AI Origin Community — Daily life and public feedback

Dominant nodes: **N1 Civic Service Port, N3 Care Crossing, N6 Community Dock**.

- S04/S06 make no-app, no-forced-profile access a baseline.
- Students/developers and long-term residents share the same public realm; developer convenience never overrides family, older-person or accessibility needs.
- Community feedback is not an “engagement event” only; it becomes a tracked input to MODIFY / STOP / CONTINUE decisions.

### Dazhongsi — Service and business validation

Dominant nodes: **N1 Civic Service Port, N6 Community Dock, selected N5 temporary service-test interface**.

- S09 treats AI-native consumption/business as a consumer-facing trial with clear terms, human service and exit/dispute channels.
- S05 makes the evening economy depend on safe lighting, walking and transport connection first, rather than a technology spectacle.

### Zhongguancun Technology Service Wing — Exchange and translation

Dominant node: **N8 Capability Interchange**.

- Capabilities are registered with version, evidence status, steward, risk class and transfer prerequisites.
- Professional services (standards, legal/IP, financing/operations interfaces as later verified) help a capability move between learning units without claiming any guaranteed policy or funding support.
- The node functions as a civic/professional interchange, not an exhibition hall.

### Xiaoyuehe Scenario Empowerment Wing — Real public-space learning

Dominant nodes: **N2 Climate Porch, N3 Care Crossing, N5 Reversible Test Yard (selective), N4 Memory Switch**.

- S02/S12 put shade, planting, seating, water and public-space continuity before active technology.
- Environmental sensing remains non-personal by default.
- The wing is a place where capabilities are tested against ordinary leisure, heat, accessibility and ecological conditions rather than isolated demonstration conditions.

## 4. East–west stitching + north–south continuity

The official taskbook requires both directions to be addressed conceptually. This workstream proposes a **two-layer continuity test** rather than a new unverified road/bridge/tunnel alignment:

### East–west stitching test

At every verified barrier crossing or neighborhood seam, Integration should check:

1. Can a person cross without entering a commercial or controlled test space?
2. Is the route physically accessible before adding digital navigation?
3. Are both sides connected to ordinary services, shade/rest and safe night movement?
4. Does a Civic Service Port or Care Crossing clarify the seam without becoming a gate?
5. If a test is nearby, is a non-participant bypass obvious and continuous?

### North–south continuity test

Along the heritage-park / Capability Backbone sequence, Integration should check:

1. Is there a continuous everyday walking/cycling/public-space experience?
2. Do the three time anchors — 1909, 2026, Future — read as a sequence without requiring an app?
3. Are Climate Porch, Memory Switch and Service Port nodes spaced according to real heat, rest, crossing and service needs rather than visual rhythm alone?
4. Are Dazhongsi, AI Origin Community and Zhongzhiyuan differentiated rather than repeated copies?
5. Does the route remain legible during digital failure?

No bridge, tunnel, road redline or engineering feasibility is asserted by these tests.

## 5. Seasonal overlays

- **Summer heat:** S02/S12 gain priority; active systems may be reduced if passive landscape performs sufficiently.
- **Rain / severe weather:** S07 continuity mode; temporary experiment equipment defaults to safe shutdown according to professional procedure.
- **School / family weekends:** S03 gains priority; test noise and circulation conflict should be reduced or tests rescheduled.
- **Major public events:** temporary crowd/service overlays require a separate safety plan; the underlying daily routes remain visible and usable.

## 6. Orchestration state machine

`CITY PROBLEM → BASELINE SPATIAL RESPONSE → CAPABILITY CANDIDATE → RISK CLASS → AUTHORIZATION → SMALL REVERSIBLE TRIAL → PUBLIC VALUE EVIDENCE → STOP / MODIFY / CONTINUE / SCALE → CROSS-ZONE REVALIDATION → ARCHIVE / RETIREMENT`

The state machine prevents a scenario card from becoming a permanent “feature list.” Every capability has a route out as well as a route in.

## Phase 4 checkpoint

- **Completed:** 12 scenarios mapped across time, five learning units, east–west stitching and north–south continuity.
- **Found:** public-space quality depends on sequencing and fallback more than on adding devices; the highest-risk scenarios are intentionally time- and space-bounded.
- **Unknown:** verified pedestrian counts, night-use patterns, thermal hotspots, accessibility discontinuities and exact operational owners.
- **Next dependency:** connect daily nodes to a three-time landmark-memory system without turning it into an exhibition route.
