# Node Typologies｜节点族谱

## Principle

A shared identity system does not mean repeated objects. Each node type has a distinct civic job, section, dwell time and failure mode. The visual family is limited to the Open Node mark, paired-track routing graphic, version/mileage tag and common status language.

## N1 — Civic Service Port｜公共服务接口

**Capabilities:** CAP-01 Find & Ask, CAP-04 Community Service.

**Use:** ask for a route, understand a service, get multilingual help, escalate to a human.

**Physical kit:** sheltered standing/seating zone; high-contrast static map; tactile/large-print layer; help contact; optional screen; paper/service directory.

**Ambient:** public service/transit status. **Consent:** saved preferences, account/service handoff, personalized recommendation. **Human override:** staff counter, phone/intercom or named service team. **Failure:** static information + human contact remains.

**Character:** small and embedded in ordinary movement; not a destination pavilion.

## N2 — Climate Porch｜气候适应廊亭

**Capability:** CAP-02 Cool & Recover.

**Use:** shade, rest, refill, recover from heat and understand safer routes.

**Physical kit:** tree/canopy shade, seating with backs/arms, drinking-water point where feasible, permeable/cool-surface strategy, planting and airflow consideration.

**Ambient:** environmental sensing and maintenance/operation coordination. **Consent:** only for a requested personalized low-exposure route. **Human override:** facilities/park operations. **Failure:** passive shade/seating/water continues.

**Character:** landscape-first; digital hardware visually subordinate.

## N3 — Care Crossing｜无障碍与照护节点

**Capability:** CAP-03 Access & Care.

**Use:** cross a difficult seam, rest, obtain accessible-route or assistance information.

**Physical kit:** continuous step-free route, tactile cues, audible/visual crossing information where relevant, rest point, clear edge protection, assistance contact.

**Ambient:** infrastructure status only. **Consent:** route preference or assistance request. **Human override:** service staff/mobility assistance. **Failure:** physical accessible path and conventional cues remain primary.

**Character:** concentrated at real discontinuities; never a decorative “accessible zone.”

## N4 — Memory Switch｜文化记忆道岔

**Capability:** CAP-05 Memory & Meaning.

**Use:** encounter verified railway/innovation history and choose deeper interpretation or simply continue daily life.

**Physical kit:** authentic material only when provenance is known; chronological/mileage markers; seating; physical archive labels; optional audio/search layer.

**Ambient:** none required. **Consent:** optional language/accessibility preference. **Human override:** curator/heritage-content review. **Failure:** physical narrative remains complete.

**Character:** quiet, material and tactile; no giant screens.

## N5 — Reversible Test Yard｜可逆城市实验站

**Capability:** CAP-06 Try & Learn.

**Use:** observe or participate in a bounded trial with visible rules and stop authority.

**Physical kit:** modular floor/service grid, removable barriers, observation edge, manual controls, posted trial card, incident/stop notice, storage/service access.

**Ambient:** trial-specific environmental/equipment status. **Consent:** required for personal participation; passersby retain a non-participating route. **Human override:** named professional operator with stop authority. **Failure:** equipment stops; ordinary route stays separated and usable.

**Character:** robust and temporary-looking by design; reversibility is visible.

## N6 — Community Dock｜社区共创节点

**Capabilities:** CAP-04 Community Service, CAP-07 Co-create & Appeal.

**Use:** propose a problem, join a workshop, inspect responses, obtain help.

**Physical kit:** shared table/room, paper input, notice/change board, workshop storage, child/older-person-friendly seating.

**Ambient:** event/status board. **Consent:** personal contact details or account-linked follow-up. **Human override:** community facilitator; original feedback remains reviewable. **Failure:** in-person meeting, paper and notice board continue.

**Character:** community scale, not corporate innovation showroom.

## N7 — Continuity Island｜应急模式节点

**Capability:** CAP-08 Emergency Continuity.

**Use:** orientation/help during outage, severe weather or service disruption.

**Physical kit:** conventional emergency map/signage, backup lighting/power for critical communications where feasible, muster/help point, manual contact list.

**Ambient:** equipment/incident status when available. **Consent:** not required for generic emergency information. **Human override:** emergency/operations command. **Failure:** analog instructions and staffed procedures are the baseline.

**Character:** calm, durable and highly legible; not branded as an AI attraction.

## N8 — Capability Interchange｜能力交换枢纽

**Capability:** CAP-09 Capability Exchange plus selected public-service functions.

**Use:** discover verified capabilities, move a capability from one learning unit to another for revalidation, connect professional/community operators.

**Physical kit:** meeting/service room, capability registry wall, open interface library, small demonstration bench only when necessary, public status board.

**Ambient:** capability registry/status sync. **Consent:** only for account/event registration. **Human override:** Public Stewardship team/designated exchange operator. **Failure:** physical registry and staffed brokerage continue.

**Character:** civic interchange rather than exhibition hall; strongest fit in Zhongguancun service wing and major Backbone junctions.

## Network placement logic

- frequent/lightweight: N1, N2, N3;
- neighborhood-scale: N4, N6, N7;
- limited/strategic: N5, N8;
- landmark anchors may combine several typologies but retain one dominant civic function.

No exact coordinates are proposed. Integration must place nodes against verified pedestrian flows, heat exposure, barriers, transit/service entries, heritage resources and ownership/operations feasibility.
