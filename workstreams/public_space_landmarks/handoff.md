## 3. Key decisions / findings

### Decision / finding A — AI must be backstage
- **evidence:** taskbook public-interest/human-review requirements; public-space design analysis; failure-mode review.
- **confidence:** high as a design decision.
- **why it matters:** prevents public space becoming an AI exhibition; shade, accessibility, lighting, wayfinding, staffing and emergency procedure remain city-design fundamentals.

### Decision / finding B — Public Capability Interface is a contract, not a kiosk
- **evidence:** all 12 scenarios require a non-AI baseline and explicit Ambient/Consent/Override status.
- **confidence:** high.
- **why it matters:** gives Integration a reusable spatial/governance interface across all five learning units without forcing visual homogeneity.

### Decision / finding C — Reuse old “Legible AI” as a sub-principle only
- **evidence:** prior candidate already had 7 personas, 12 scenarios, L0–L3 logic, human review/fallback and 5 public-space concepts.
- **confidence:** high for the audited branch content.
- **why it matters:** preserves mature work while respecting the locked upgraded constitution: City OS / Capability Backbone / Public Capability Interface / Landmark as Memory.

### Decision / finding D — Landmark should record learning, not only achievement
- **evidence:** locked design constitution requires Landmark as Memory, STOP/MODIFY/CONTINUE/SCALE, Annual City Version Release and retirement.
- **confidence:** high.
- **why it matters:** makes failure, correction, stopping and technological exit visible signs of governance maturity.

### Decision / finding E — 1909→2026→Future is one sequence, not three icon buildings
- **evidence:** official Beijing historical/public-space sources support 1909 railway memory and the heritage-park role; current design constitution requires time continuity.
- **confidence:** high for narrative structure; low/proposed for exact physical anchors.
- **why it matters:** prevents “landmark competition” and links memory to everyday public space and City OS learning.

### Decision / finding F — Future must include reset/removal capacity
- **evidence:** reversible-test logic and retirement requirement.
- **confidence:** high as a design principle.
- **why it matters:** a city cannot credibly claim reversible experimentation if it designs only installation/activation and not shutdown/removal.

### Decision / finding G — Latest 2026 park context reinforces continuity but is not competition geometry
- **evidence:** Beijing Municipal Forestry and Parks Bureau 2026-07-13 Phase II supporting-project update describes north–south / east–west slow-mobility linkage and mixed daily-life functions.
- **confidence:** high for the published project update.
- **why it matters:** strengthens the real-world relevance of the continuity/public-life approach while remaining separate from official competition boundary/control data.
