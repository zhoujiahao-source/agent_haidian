# Integration Crosswalk — AI Governance & Metrics

Status: **handoff aid only; not a canonical submission edit**

This crosswalk makes the governance workstream easy to integrate without turning it into a separate, detached AI-governance chapter. It preserves the Word prompt's requirement that the three positionings, five functions and three-areas/two-wings logic remain visible while this Agent stays within its role boundary.

## 1. Three positioning statements

| fixed positioning | governance/metrics support | integration hook |
|---|---|---|
| 百年京张文化带 | `Landmark as Memory`, STOP/retirement records and Annual City Version Release turn urban learning into a traceable public memory rather than a technology-success monument | Public-space / landmark narrative should show learning, correction and retirement as part of the century-scale story |
| 都市 AI 生活体验带 | Ambient Layer / Consent Layer, human override, public dashboard and subgroup public-value review make AI experience legible, optional where appropriate and accountable | Scenario cards and public-space interfaces should expose risk, owner, data category, feedback and exit route |
| AI 融合创新带 | Urban Learning Protocol + Capability Backbone provides the mechanism for capabilities to be produced, tested, transferred, revalidated and retired across locations | Structure/operations modules should show exchange and revalidation rather than identical AI deployment everywhere |

## 5. Machine-readable integration candidates

- `metric_proposals.json`: schema-compatible custom metrics; keep all operational values `unknown/null` until real records exist.
- `schemas/eligible_issue.schema.json`: intake/eligibility object used before an issue may enter a UAR cohort.
- `schemas/experiment_card.schema.json`: minimum bounded-experiment registration contract.
- `schemas/capability_record.schema.json`: evidence envelope for Capability Backbone exchange.
- `schemas/city_version_release.schema.json`: annual added/modified/scaled/stopped/unresolved delta.
- `dashboard_data_contract.json`: public/audit disclosure boundary.

## 6. Non-negotiable integration rules

1. Do not convert City Agent detection volume into success or into the UAR denominator.
2. Do not treat Responsible STOP as adapted public problem; report it as learning/safety outcome.
3. Do not allow high model capability or source-area maturity to relax human authorization tier.
4. Do not award target-area transfer credit until target baseline, risk re-tiering, local authorization and bounded revalidation exist.
5. Do not publish a composite index until its component rubric and evidence are visible.
6. Keep unknown operational values unknown; formulas are not measurements.
7. Keep provisional geometry explicitly provisional and separate from statutory claims.
