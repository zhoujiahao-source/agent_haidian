# Open Node Logo System

## Core idea

`Open Node` is not a generic AI symbol. It is a public interface / station / exchange point with a deliberate opening. The mark must remain identifiable in monochrome and at 16–24 px.

## Geometry family A — Open Ring Switch (recommended)

Construction on a 24 × 24 unit grid:

- draw a ring centered at (12,12), outer diameter 16, stroke 2;
- remove a 4-unit arc centered on the east-northeast quadrant (approximately 20°–55°);
- extend two parallel 2-unit-stroke tracks from x=2 to the left edge of the ring at y=9 and y=15;
- inside the opening, one track continues outward and one bends by 30° to create a route choice;
- maintain round line caps only at the external/open ends; use butt caps at structural joins.

Meaning: station + route choice + unfinished/open system.

Strengths: strongest small-size recognition; easiest to animate as route exchange; scales to district variants.

## Geometry family B — Open Interchange

Construction:

- two parallel vertical tracks spaced 5 units apart;
- a circular node of 10 units intersects them;
- node boundary is removed on north and south to make the lines visibly pass through;
- a single diagonal switch leg exits at 45°.

Meaning: capability flows do not terminate at the logo; they pass through and exchange.

Strengths: strongest Capability Backbone reading; good on maps.

Risk: can resemble transit branding if stripped of labels.

## Geometry family C — Split Milepost Node

Construction:

- open square/circle hybrid node with one missing corner;
- two short parallel ticks enter from the west;
- a version/milepost notch sits on the lower edge;
- east opening remains visibly larger than any decorative cut.

Meaning: versioned public infrastructure / milepost / open access.

Strengths: best for Annual City Version Release.

Risk: slightly weaker at 16 px than family A.

## Recommendation

Use **Family A** as primary mark, Family B as map/network glyph and Family C as version/archive secondary device.

## Clear space

Let `x` equal the primary stroke width. Minimum clear space around primary logo: `4x`; for public signage use `6x` where possible.

## Minimum size

- digital standalone symbol: 20 px preferred, 16 px absolute minimum;
- print standalone symbol: 5 mm preferred, 4 mm absolute minimum;
- bilingual lockup: 28 mm minimum width in print.

At minimum size: remove all optional inner ticks; never thin the stroke below optical legibility.

## Monochrome

Primary logo must work as 100% K / single ink. The opening must be defined by actual negative space, never only by a color change.

## Five-zone extension

Do not create five unrelated logos. Keep the same Open Node geometry and add a small route-index element:

- Z1 众智园: north switch / `01`
- Z2 AI 原点社区: center-open / `02`
- Z3 大钟寺: south switch / `03`
- W1 中关村科技服务翼: west exchange / `W1`
- W2 小月河场景赋能翼: east exchange / `W2`

District identity uses index + label + local capability subtitle, not new pictograms.

## Motion principle

A 1.2–2.0 s loop may show:

1. two tracks enter;
2. node outline draws but leaves the opening;
3. one route switches;
4. both routes exit;
5. motion stops on the static mark.

No pulsing brain, particle cloud or glowing AI core.

## Logo audit against 10 questions

- open not closed: YES by negative-space opening;
- station/interface/exchange: YES;
- avoids generic AI symbol: YES;
- 16–24 px: YES, Family A strongest;
- monochrome: YES;
- extensible to five zones: YES via index system;
- route/switch animation: YES;
- railway DNA logical: YES, track/station/switch grammar;
- durable public-service context: YES;
- avoids startup look: YES if paired with restrained typography and no gradient/glow.
