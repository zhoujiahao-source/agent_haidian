## Recommended naming architecture

Do not force every concept into the master name. A durable hierarchy is:

- **Master public name:** short geographic + civic identity;
- **System descriptor:** `Urban Capability Exchange Network / 城市能力交换网络`;
- **spatial backbone:** `Capability Backbone / 城市能力交换骨干`;
- **public interface:** `Open Node / 开放节点`;
- **governance sub-principle:** `Legible AI / 可读 AI`;
- **annual label:** `Jing-Zhang City v2027`.

## Strong candidate

`京张·城市能力交换带 / Jing-Zhang Urban Capability Exchange`

Why: it directly expresses the twenty-year legacy and the exchange-network logic without locking the identity to a specific generation of AI technology.

Keep `京张·可读AI城市带 / Jing-Zhang Legible AI Belt` as the current proposal title, but reposition `可读 AI / Legible AI` as a governance and visual principle while the narrative headline becomes City OS / capability exchange.
