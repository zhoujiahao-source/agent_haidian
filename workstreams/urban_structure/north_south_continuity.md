# North-South Continuity — From Greenway to Urban Sequence

## 1. Continuity definition

North-south continuity means that a person can experience a coherent chain of public life, culture, innovation, service and landscape along the Jing-Zhang corridor. It is not defined by a single uninterrupted green line.

The existing `ROAD-001` is retained as a **candidate continuity axis**. Its geometry remains a design proposal pending verified existing routes and heritage constraints.

## 2. Five-segment experience sequence

### Segment N1 — Research & Verification

**Primary host:** Zhongzhiyuan learning unit.

**Spatial actions:** porous campus edge; accessible slow-mobility route; public observation terrace; controlled test court; developer commons; everyday food/rest edge; memory/version marker.

**Daily-life test:** a resident can pass through without entering a test zone; a researcher can reach a controlled test environment without mixing experimental circulation with ordinary public movement.

### Segment N2 — Blue-Green / Open-Life Transition

**Primary host:** relationship between northern innovation areas and Xiaoyuehe-oriented public-life testing.

**Spatial actions:** shade/rest sequence, ecological edge, low-sensing refuge, climate adaptation pilots, public toilets/water/wayfinding where feasible, removable low-risk interface points.

**Daily-life test:** the space remains useful as a park/public route when every digital system is offline.

### Segment N3 — Neighborhood Learning

**Primary host:** Beijing AI Origin Community.

**Spatial actions:** civic room, community-service interface, small plazas/courtyards, analog service route, accessible neighborhood links, low-threshold feedback points, `AI Mile Zero` reinterpreted as a learning/memory node rather than an AI monument.

**Daily-life test:** older people, children and users without smartphones can obtain the same essential route/service information through human or physical interfaces.

### Segment N4 — Exchange & Memory

**Primary host:** central exchange sequence between learning units.

**Spatial actions:** City Version Release display, contributor / public-value record, Retired Capability Archive element, flexible assembly space, public review room, clear branch routes toward the Two Wings.

**Daily-life test:** the space works as a normal square/park/street edge outside annual events.

### Segment N5 — Market, Transit & Night

**Primary host:** Dazhongsi learning unit.

**Spatial actions:** transit-market service commons, flexible storefront/service bays, shaded waiting, night seating, culture/event spill-out, service-recovery/human-help point, public interpretation of tested services.

**Daily-life test:** normal consumption and transit remain possible without interacting with AI systems; experiments do not occupy the only public route.

## 3. Continuity through time

A continuous city experience also needs temporal programming:

| Time | Backbone emphasis | Spatial implication |
|---|---|---|
| morning | commute, school, exercise, maintenance | clear routes, low visual clutter, accessibility support |
| midday | work exchange, park use, community service | shaded seating, civic rooms, service nodes |
| evening | market, culture, learning events | flexible spill-out, safe lighting, transit clarity |
| night | return trips, limited night economy, maintenance | safe core route, human help, reduced experimental activity |
| annual cycle | City Learning Assembly / Version Release | event-ready spaces without permanently blocking daily use |

## 4. Continuity through capability exchange

The sequence should make different capability flows spatially understandable:

- Zhongzhiyuan → technical validation evidence;
- AI Origin → social/public-service feedback;
- Dazhongsi → market/service evidence;
- Zhongguancun Wing → legal/IP/standard/capital/professional support;
- Xiaoyuehe Wing → real public-space / resilience learning.

Wayfinding and public information can borrow railway station/switch/mileage logic to show **where a capability came from, where it is being tested, its status, and how to opt out or find human help**.

## 5. Minimum continuity kit

A segment is not considered “continuous” merely because a line connects geometrically. Final plan review should check:

- step-free route or documented gap;
- safe crossing or documented Unknown;
- rest/shade intervals appropriate to context;
- clear physical wayfinding;
- ordinary daily destination/service relationship;
- no-digital alternative;
- human help/escalation point at higher-intensity nodes;
- maintenance responsibility concept;
- night condition where relevant.

## Phase 4 conclusion

The north-south structure becomes a **series of different public-life rooms linked by a common capability-exchange grammar**. This gives the Backbone identity without making the entire 11.4 km² design area look or operate the same.
