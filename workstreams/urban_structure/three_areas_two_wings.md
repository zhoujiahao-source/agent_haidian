# Three Areas / Two Wings — Capability-to-Space Mapping

The official task roles are fixed. This file deepens them into differentiated learning-unit prototypes without claiming parcel-level planning decisions.

## 1. Zhongzhiyuan AI Autonomous Innovation Acceleration Area / 众智园

- **Primary capability:** technical capability production + controlled validation.
- **Secondary capabilities:** AI governance test protocols; developer/research exchange.
- **Target users:** researchers, startup teams, safety evaluators, developers, professional reviewers, visiting public groups.
- **Spatial prototype:** **Porous Verification Campus / 开放验证园** — shared test halls/courtyards, publicly observable but safety-separated test loops, developer commons, review rooms and a civic-facing interpretation edge.
- **Public-space action:** replace the image of a closed research enclave with multiple public thresholds: observation terrace, shared forum, everyday food/rest/service edge and controlled test entrance.
- **What it sends into Backbone:** validated capability modules, test evidence, safety limits, model/service version information.
- **What it receives:** real public problems, community feedback, professional/legal constraints, market/service signals.
- **Risk logic:** L2/L3 experiments require time/space limits, safety perimeter, human override and explicit status; everyday public areas remain L0/L1.
- **Do not claim:** exact campus boundary, building demolition, road closure, construction scale or infrastructure capacity.

## 2. Beijing AI Origin Community / 北京 AI 原点社区

- **Primary capability:** social feedback + talent community + public-service learning.
- **Secondary capabilities:** open-source co-creation; inclusion/accessibility learning.
- **Target users:** residents, students, researchers, families, older people, children, people with disabilities, community workers.
- **Spatial prototype:** **Learning Neighborhood / 城市学习街区** — small civic rooms, neighborhood service interfaces, mixed daily-life streets, analog alternatives, community courtyards and a network of low-threshold feedback points.
- **Public-space action:** make AI service learning happen at ordinary places such as entrances, transit transfer, community service, park edges and ground-floor shared rooms — not only in an exhibition center.
- **What it sends into Backbone:** problem definitions, service feedback, inclusion findings, complaint/failure patterns, public-value evaluation.
- **What it receives:** validated low-risk capabilities, professional support, cultural programming and resources from other units.
- **Risk logic:** personal recognition/recommendation/personalization is Consent Layer; no-digital route and human desk remain available.
- **Do not claim:** that residents automatically consent, that community data can be freely harvested, or that an AI system has decision authority.

## 3. Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster / 大钟寺

- **Primary capability:** market + AI-native service validation.
- **Secondary capabilities:** culture/night economy interaction; public-service/service-recovery testing.
- **Target users:** commuters, consumers, small businesses, service workers, visitors, residents, entrepreneurs.
- **Spatial prototype:** **Transit-Market Service Commons / 交通市场服务场** — active street edges, flexible storefront/service bays, night-use public spaces, service recovery/help desk and visible prototype zones integrated with normal commerce.
- **Public-space action:** test whether AI-native services improve a real trip, purchase, accessibility need or service job; avoid an “AI mall” where technology is the destination.
- **What it sends into Backbone:** demand signals, usability evidence, service/business model results, workforce impacts, failure and recovery records.
- **What it receives:** tested technical capabilities, legal/IP/professional support, community feedback and culture/event content.
- **Risk logic:** commercial experimentation must not turn public passage into forced consent or data capture; human service alternatives remain visible.
- **Do not claim:** specific tenant, investment, mall redevelopment, station construction or final operating entity.

## 4. Zhongguancun Technology Service Wing / 中关村科技服务翼

- **Primary capability:** innovation-resource exchange — IP, capital, standards, legal/professional services and global linkages.
- **Secondary capabilities:** project brokerage; developer/business support.
- **Target users:** startups, researchers, investors, professional-service teams, institutions, international collaborators.
- **Spatial prototype:** **Professional Exchange Interface / 专业服务交换界面** — distributed service rooms, meeting/collaboration spaces, demo/review rooms and public-facing knowledge desks connected to normal streets/transit.
- **Public-space action:** make professional services spatially accessible to the other learning units rather than treating the wing as a transport corridor.
- **What it sends into Backbone:** IP/legal/standard/finance/service packages and partnership pathways.
- **What it receives:** test results, problems, prototypes and demand from the three areas and Xiaoyuehe wing.
- **Risk logic:** no claim that capital/service support equals public approval; high-impact decisions remain institutionally accountable.

## 5. Xiaoyuehe Scenario Empowerment Wing / 小月河场景赋能翼

- **Primary capability:** real public-space and urban-scenario testing.
- **Secondary capabilities:** blue-green resilience; daily wellness/accessibility/public-life learning.
- **Target users:** residents, park users, commuters, children, older people, people with disabilities, maintenance teams, visitors.
- **Spatial prototype:** **Blue-Green Living Test Field / 蓝绿公共生活试验场** — accessible paths, shade/rest nodes, ecological edges, removable low-risk test infrastructure and sensor-light refuge spaces.
- **Public-space action:** ordinary park/water-edge life remains primary; AI capabilities are evaluated by whether they improve comfort, access, maintenance, safety or climate response.
- **What it sends into Backbone:** real-world low-risk performance, maintenance feedback, inclusion findings and environmental/public-space learning.
- **What it receives:** technical prototypes, professional review, community problem statements and operational support.
- **Risk logic:** public/aggregate/minimum-necessary data only; no default individual tracking; pilots are reversible.
- **Do not claim:** precise water/park boundaries, hydrological engineering feasibility or sensor deployment entitlement.

## Exchange matrix

| Unit | Produces / validates | Receives | Distinct physical signature |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zhongzhiyuan | technical modules, safety/test evidence | problems, feedback, rules, market signals | porous verification campus + controlled test commons |
| AI Origin Community | social feedback, inclusion/public-service learning | validated capabilities, resources | learning neighborhood + civic interfaces |
| Dazhongsi | market/service evidence, service recovery lessons | technical capability + community/professional inputs | transit-market service commons + night public life |
| Zhongguancun Wing | IP/capital/legal/standard/professional support | prototypes + test evidence + needs | distributed professional exchange interfaces |
| Xiaoyuehe Wing | real public-space/climate/maintenance evidence | prototypes + review + community needs | blue-green living test field + analog refuge |

## Phase 2 conclusion

The five units are intentionally non-homogeneous. Their common language is the Capability Backbone and Public Capability Interface, but each one has a different production/validation role and a different urban-space prototype. This avoids turning the entire belt into repeated “AI parks”.
